# #661 review pilot: negative, 4 of 8

#661 asked whether a fresh-context reviewer subagent, handed the child's
design before it wrote code, makes a dispatched child's work better. The bar
was declared before any pair ran: the reviewed arm wins **at least 80% of the
decided pairs, with at least 6 decided**; anything else removes the feature,
with no second round.

The reviewed arm won **4 of 8** decided pairs (50%). The tool that reads the
twins log prints **5 of 8** (62%), for the reason given under *The a9b631
correction*. The bar fails with either number, so the feature was removed
(#682).

## How it ran

- 10 pairs from `mnemo dispatch N --twins --arms`: 5 on mnemo, 5 on
  mnemo-desktop. Each pair worked one real bug, filed for the run in neutral
  words (no "pilot", no "review"). In each pair one twin, drawn at random,
  had the design checkpoint; the other had the plain prompt. The pre-PR
  checkpoint was not in the pilot: its offline gate caught 0 of 10 known bugs
  (`2026-10-14-review-gate-661.md`).
- Each pair was judged by fresh-context model judges that saw the issue and
  the two diffs as `mnemo twins show` printed them, closing reports
  stripped, and could run either diff in a scratch copy. Each pair was judged
  twice, the second time with A and B swapped. A pair is won only when both
  orders pick the same diff; otherwise it is a tie.

mapped back to those shown labels. Tokens are each twin's output tokens at
the first `show`; findings are the reviewer findings the reviewed twin

## The a9b631 correction

The twins log keeps the **first** `twins prefer` answer for a pair, by
design: `prefer` refuses a second one, because an answer given after the
labels are known is no longer blind. For a9b631 the first answer, `A` at
01:11Z on 2026-10-15, was recorded by accident, from a pasted command line.
Shown A was the reviewed twin, so the log counts the pair as a review win.

Both judge orders picked shown **B**, the control. By the protocol above the
pair is a control win. The other nine logged answers were written from the
judges' consensus, and each agrees with it, so a9b631 is the only pair where
the two sources differ, and the one point between 5/8 and 4/8.

To rebuild both numbers from what ships:

- **4/8** from `2026-10-15-review-pilot-661-judges.json`: for each pair, if
  both `judges[].verdict_shown` are the same label, the winner is
  `shown[label]`; otherwise a tie. Count the review and control winners.
- **5/8** from the twins log: `python3 tools/measure_review_pilot.py`, or
  `shown[logged_choice]` in the same JSON, which is the log's first answer.

## Cost

- Output tokens: reviewed arm **371,295**, control **213,745**: **+74%**.
- Wall time to the first `show`, summed: reviewed 372 min, control 325 min.
  The reviewed side includes a `cargo test` that hung for 33 minutes (see
  caveats).
- The reviewer raised 23 findings over the 10 reviewed twins. The children
  accepted all 23 and rejected none.

The winners won on concrete grounds that showed up for both arms: staying
compatible with an older daemon, not freezing the page, not leaving a lock
held forever.

## Caveats

- 10 pairs cannot rule out a small effect. The bar was fixed in advance for
  that reason, and a negative here means "not the large effect the cost
  needs", not "no effect".
- The judge was a model, not the maintainer. The protocol changed mid-pilot,
  after batch 1, to take the reading off a human session that had seen the
  children's notices.
- Batch 1 (ed5ec5, 9a2ac3, 7fff3c): the children's closing reports reached
  the dispatching session through child notices, and two of them named the
  review. The judges never saw those reports. Batch 1 went 2 review, 1
  control; without it, the reviewed arm won 2 of 5.
- One intervention: in 5ba03b (mnemo-desktop#407), a `cargo test` in the
  reviewed twin hung for 33 minutes and was killed, with no message to the
  child. Control won that pair.
- In both ties each order's judge picked whichever diff it was handed as A
  (shown A, then shown B once swapped): the pick followed the position, not
  the diff.

## Outcome

Removed in #682: the `dispatch.review` key (ignored if still set), `mnemo
dispatch --review/--no-review` and `--twins --arms`, the `mnemo-reviewer`
subagent, `mnemo review record` and its ledger writer, and the arm handling
in `twins show`/`prefer`. `tools/measure_review_gate.py` and
`tools/measure_review_pilot.py` keep what they need and still reproduce their
numbers. All 10 winners were opened as PRs: #677–#681 and
xyrlan/mnemo-desktop#416–#420.
