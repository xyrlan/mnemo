- **`tools/measure_session_states.py` counts the `(state, tempo)` pairs in Claude Code's job records, and `Session.is_blocked` stays on `tempo` alone.** #737 asked whether `state=blocked, tempo=active` is a question the queue cannot see. It is not. Claude Code writes that pair when an answer reaches a session: `tempo` goes to `active`, the answer becomes `detail`, and `state` stays `blocked` until the turn ends. A plain-text question is written as `tempo=blocked`, which the queue already shows. Counting `state` would have put a session working on the maintainer's answer under WAITING ON YOU. The tool prints every pair, and it checks each `state`-only blocked record's `detail` against the last human turn in its transcript. On the maintainer's machine (228 records) the one such record is the maintainer's own prompt, written 0.62s after it was typed. The `answer-keeps-blocked-state` assumption records the writer's shapes. The opt-in live test re-checks them against the installed binary, without spawning a session. (#737)
