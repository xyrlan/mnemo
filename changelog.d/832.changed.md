- **A child dispatched with `--advisor` (or a piece's `advisor:`) is now told when to consult it.** The first four advised children (#818) had the tool and made no call, so the pilot measured Sonnet alone against Opus. The child's prompt now asks it to consult before settling its plan, after hitting the same error twice, and on the finished, committed diff before its closing report. Before each call it writes out what it decided and why, what it ruled out and the evidence, because the advisor reads the conversation and takes no input. If the advisor disagrees, the child resolves that before going on. Its closing report ends on an `Advisor:` paragraph saying how many times it consulted and what changed. Each call re-reads the whole conversation at the advisor's price, so the checkpoints are a cost you choose with the flag. On one check dispatch, a Sonnet child made a single call on its finished diff, which cost $0.44 at Opus list price and was 53% of the child's cost. Without an advisor the prompt is byte-identical, and a Grok child never gets the paragraph. In a `--twins --advisor` pair the two arms now differ by model, advisor and this paragraph. Each arm's prompt sha256 is recorded with the pair, and `mnemo twins show` cuts the `Advisor:` account and any other line naming the advisor from both closing reports alike. `tools/measure_advisor_pairs.py` adds each advised twin's advisor share of its cost and marks the pairs dispatched before the paragraph.
