- **`tools/measure_briefing_value.py` asks whether the right briefing helps a session pick up its work, and whether the wrong one hurts.** For the 110 sessions where #534's two raters agree a right briefing exists, it answers the first typed prompt on the session's own model three ways — the right briefing, none, and the hook's pick where that was not right — each inside the SessionStart envelope as the hook builds it today, under #533's cap. Two blind raters who never see a briefing judge each reply against what the session actually did next, and mark wrong assumptions about the state of the work. It reports `h_right`, `h_wrong`, wrong-state rates and the raters' kappa with bootstrap CIs over sessions, against a bar set before measuring; every answer is cached so a rerun resumes. (#540)
