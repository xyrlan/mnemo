- **`mnemo dispatch --review` gives each child a fresh-context reviewer, off by default and piloted before it can become the default.** The child hands its design to a read-only subagent on its own model, after exploring and before writing code, and only when the change touches persisted data or a schema, a public API or CLI, a UI flow, or concurrency/scale. Otherwise it records `design checkpoint: skipped, no design decision`. The reviewer judges against the project's own written standards (vault rules, `CLAUDE.md`, precedent cited as `file:line`); unanchored taste and wider work go to suggestion lists. The child accepts or rejects each finding with a reason, and `mnemo review record` keeps one ledger row per finding in `<vault>/.mnemo/review-ledger.jsonl` and prints the `## Review checkpoints` section for the PR body. The pre-PR checkpoint is left out: the offline gate (`tools/measure_review_gate.py`, base-and-head-only snapshots of past child PRs) found the known bug in 0 of 10 re-verified candidates against a bar of 4. `mnemo dispatch <n> --twins --arms` runs the A/B pilot, one twin reviewed and one not, and `mnemo twins show` stays blind by stripping the review section and refusing on any leftover marker. `tools/measure_review_pilot.py` prints the verdict: the switch defaults on only if the review arm wins at least 80% of at least 6 decided pairs, and anything else removes the feature. `dispatch.review` in the config turns it on for every dispatch.
