- **The suite's timing tests no longer fail under `pytest-xdist` load.** The
  reflex index's linearity test counts the lines of mnemo's code a build runs
  instead of timing two builds, so it gives the same answer on any load: a
  vault 8x larger runs 7.99x the lines, and the #610 quadratic merge, injected
  back, runs 29x and fails it. The BM25F p95 budget measures the scorer's CPU
  time instead of wall time, so a worker descheduled by its neighbours no
  longer counts against it; a scorer slow on 3 calls in 30 still fails it.
  Four hang guards became deterministic too: the Grok roster read and the
  sync pull at SessionStart are checked by what they waited on, not by a
  stopwatch (one failed at 7.2 s of a 5 s bound in a local `-n auto` run), and
  day one's slow-judge stub blocks until released instead of sleeping 0.3 s,
  which a starved thread could outwait. No `serial` marker or second CI pass
  was needed. (#750)
