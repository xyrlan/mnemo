- **A child dispatched from a `bypassPermissions` session now runs `bypassPermissions` too, so its finish notice reaches you as a turn without asking for approval.** Children ran `auto` only because that is `claude --bg`'s default, and a bypass session holds a notice from a child in another permission class. Until now the only fix was `"crossSessionInbound": "accept"` in your own settings, which lets any process running as you prompt your bypass sessions. That setting can now come out. A child of any other session, or of a plain terminal, still runs `auto`: an unattended `default` child would stop at its first prompt. `--may` still decides what a child may publish, a `--read-only` child still has no Edit, Write or NotebookEdit (measured under bypass), and a woken child keeps its mode. If a settings file mnemo can read disables bypass, the child runs `auto` and the dispatch report says why on a `mode:` line. `dispatch.childMode: "auto"` keeps every child in `auto`. `mnemo doctor` now names these levers alongside `accept`. `tools/measure_child_mode.py` measures it on throwaway sessions.
